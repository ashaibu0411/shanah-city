# Shanah City — PRODUCT.md (agent-facing)

## Users

Church members, volunteers, leaders, and staff on web and mobile (Capacitor). Many flows are on phones during Sunday services.

## Product purpose

Help people connect with Shanah City: worship, community, giving, kids check-in, groups, messaging, leadership resources.

## Accessibility & inclusion

- Readable type at arm’s length on mobile
- Reduced motion respected
- Clear labels for forms (kids ministry, giving, profile)

## Design principles

1. Warm and trustworthy — not startup hype
2. One primary action per screen in operational flows (check-in, giving)
3. Authentic imagery over decorative UI chrome
