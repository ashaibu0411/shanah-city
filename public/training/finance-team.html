Finance team

Main path: Admin (⚙) · Group: Finance Team

Finance access
+ group leader

What this helps you achieve

  • Record weekly offering counts digitally — no lost paper sheets
  • Track gifts and send thank-you messages from one system
  • Give leadership a clear, exportable giving picture

Admin → Finance

  • Enter weekly counts by fund and method
  • Save drafts and submit weeks
  • Run date-range reports
  • Export CSV for records

Admin → Giving

  • Record individual gifts
  • Filter and review giving history
  • Send thank-you messages
  • Generate guest giving statements

Admin → Overview

  • See giving totals and related metrics at a glance

Monthly accountability

  • Groups → Finance Team → Report
  • Group leader submits the monthly finance report
  • Due by the 5th for the prior month

Important note

  • Finance can submit count sheets
  • Only Admin Group can reopen a submitted week
