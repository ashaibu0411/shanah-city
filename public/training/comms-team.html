Comms team

Admins: Admin → Comms · Everyone: Profile → Comms request

Admin Group
runs comms

What this helps you achieve

  • Plan announcements across email, social, app banner, and push
  • Turn ministry requests into approved, scheduled publicity
  • Promote finished work to the live app when it is ready

Admin → Comms (admins)

  • Manage the comms calendar by channel and week
  • Review ministry comms requests
  • Approve and schedule items
  • Promote finished work to the app
  • Generate social post helpers for calendar items

Any team member

  • Profile → Comms request
  • Submit requests for graphics, copy, email, social, banners, push
  • Track status of your own requests

Channels you can plan

  • Email · Social media · App banner · Push notification · Other ministry publicity

Training tip

  • Ministries submit requests early in the week
  • Admins approve and slot them on the comms calendar before Sunday
