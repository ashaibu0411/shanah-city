Grow & connect

  • Media / Sermons — messages and clips

  • Groups — join ministries, chat, polls
  • Messages — private member chat

Sunday & church life

  • Calendar — events and RSVP
  • Meetings — Shift Your Morning/Evening links
  • Check-in — kids and FrontLiners arrival
  • Give — online giving
  • Photos — church gallery

Profile & settings

  • Profile — family tree, giving history, RSVPs
  • Push notifications — devotions, messages, worship, kids
  • Comms request — ask for publicity help
  • Background theme — light or dark (More menu)

Guests & visitors

  • Plan a visit — connect with the church
  • Guest cards go to the Follow-Up team
  • Sign up to become a member and join groups

Getting started (tell new members)

  • Download the app · Create an account · Turn on notifications
  • Join your ministry in Groups
  • Complete Before You Serve if your team requires it
