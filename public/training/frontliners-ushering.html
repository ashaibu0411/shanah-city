FrontLiners & ushering

FrontLiners hub · Check-in → FrontLiners

Two related
groups

What this helps you achieve

  • Know who is serving as ushers and greeters each Sunday
  • Track volunteer arrival on Sunday morning
  • Replace paper usher lists with a live schedule

FrontLiners group — members

  • Log volunteer check-in on Sunday
  • View published usher and greeter schedules
  • Use FrontLiners tab or Check-in

FrontLiners group leader

  • Build assignments by role (lead, usher, greeter)
  • Publish and unpublish schedules
  • Copy schedules between services

Ushering Ministry group leader

  • Groups → Ushering Ministry → Manage
  • Publish service rosters
  • Report tab — monthly Ushering report

Know the difference

  • FrontLiners = Sunday arrival + usher scheduling hub
  • Ushering Ministry = service rosters + monthly ushering report
  • FrontLiners group does not file a monthly leader report in the app
