Any ministry
group leader

What this helps you achieve

  • Run your team (roster, chat, events) in one place
  • Report monthly to pastors without paper forms
  • Keep members connected between Sundays

Manage tab

  • Add and remove regular members
  • Require re-training when needed
  • Publish service rosters (where your ministry uses them)
  • Promote leaders and assistant leaders
  • Change group icon

Report · Events · Polls

  • Report — monthly Ministry Leader Report
  • Due by the 5th for the prior month
  • Events — create events and RSVPs
  • Polls — create group polls (leader only)

Dashboard & chat

  • Quick actions: chat, calendar, roster, invite, report
  • Ministry-specific links (Worship, FrontLiners, guests, etc.)
  • Group messaging for your team

Reportable ministries

  • Choir, Kids, Teens, Ushering, Ladies, Men, Follow-Up, Prayer, Finance, Media, and most ministry groups
  • Not FrontLiners, Admin Group, Team ZNCF
