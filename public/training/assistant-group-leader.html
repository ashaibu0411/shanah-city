Assistant group leader

Groups → [your ministry] → Manage

Assistant
leader role

What this helps you achieve

  • Help the primary leader run day-to-day team operations
  • Manage members and rosters without full leadership authority

Assistant leader CAN

  • Open the Manage tab
  • Add and remove regular members (not leaders)
  • Publish service rosters
  • View training completion and require training
  • Help with Power Couples resources (where applicable)

Assistant leader CANNOT

  • Promote or demote leaders or assistant leaders
  • Change the group icon
  • Create group polls
  • Create or edit group events
  • Submit the monthly Report tab (leader only)

When to escalate

  • Leadership changes → ask the primary group leader
  • Monthly report due → primary leader submits from the Report tab
  • New events or polls → primary leader creates them
