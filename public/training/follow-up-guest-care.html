Follow-Up
Ministry

What this helps you achieve

  • Work new visitors within 48 hours
  • Track contact status so no guest falls through the cracks
  • See every guest card from connect and visit flows in one queue

Follow-up team members

  • Open the guest queue
  • View new visitor submissions
  • Update status (contacted, returned, etc.)
  • Work cards from connect / plan-a-visit flows

Follow-up group leader

  • Same guest queue access
  • Manage team membership and rosters
  • Report tab — Follow-Up & Care monthly report
  • Due by the 5th for the prior month

Where guests come from

  • Plan a visit and connect forms on the app and website
  • Guest cards land in the queue for your team to action
Training tip: Check the guest queue at least once daily, especially Monday after Sunday services.
