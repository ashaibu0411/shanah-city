Team ZNCF
writers

What this helps you achieve

  • Publish daily devotions on a schedule with push to the whole church
  • Include audio and artwork for a rich daily experience
  • Schedule go-live times so devotions publish automatically

What writers can do

  • Draft and edit devotion content
  • Set publish date and time (auto go-live)
  • Add audio recordings and artwork
  • Tag devotions (e.g. couples content)
  • Share devotion links with the team

What members see

  • Daily devotion on the Devotions tab
  • Push notification at 7:30 AM Denver (when scheduled)
  • Read, listen, react, and mark as read

Before you start

  • Must be a member of Team ZNCF (approval required to join)
  • Open Write Devotions from the app menu or More sheet
